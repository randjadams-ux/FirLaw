Civil rights · Federal court

Federal court orders Oregon to provide access to the voters’ pamphlet.

Fir Law Group represented Mary Martin in civil-rights litigation against the State of Oregon. After she was unable to submit a voters’ pamphlet statement because of her financial status and disabilities, Fir Law Group filed suit.

The federal court granted a restraining order and injunction against the Oregon Secretary of State. Ms. Martin’s argument was ultimately published in the voters’ pamphlet.
